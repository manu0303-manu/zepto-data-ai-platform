QUERY 6 - JOIN
============================================================
SQL:
SELECT
    books.title,
    books.price_gbp,
    books.price_inr,
    categories.category_name
FROM books
JOIN categories
    ON books.category_id = categories.category_id
ORDER BY books.price_gbp DESC
